How to play
  • Tap a tile in the same row or column as the gap to slide it.
  • Tap a tile further away and every tile between it and the gap slides together, as one move.
  • Arrow keys slide the tile next to the gap in that direction.
  • Put the numbers back in order, reading left to right, with the gap in the bottom-right corner.
